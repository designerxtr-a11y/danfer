## 2. Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca) Full-Day Trek from Cusco

**Short summary:**
Hike to the surreal Rainbow Mountain of Vinicunca at 5,200 m and witness its naturally striped slopes of red, gold and turquoise. An early start from Cusco, breakfast and lunch included, with the snow-capped Ausangate as your backdrop.

**Highlights:**
- Reach the famous Rainbow Mountain at 5,200 m above sea level
- Breakfast and lunch included
- Sweeping views of the sacred Ausangate glacier
- Optional horseback ride available to ease the climb
- Professional guide and round-trip transport from your hotel

**Full description:**
One of the most jaw-dropping landscapes on Earth awaits on this full-day trek to Vinicunca, the Rainbow Mountain. We pick you up early from Cusco and drive through dramatic Andean scenery and traditional villages to the trailhead. After a hearty breakfast, you'll hike across high-altitude plateaus dotted with alpacas and llamas, climbing steadily toward the ridge.

When you crest the final pass, the multicolored mountain reveals itself — mineral-rich layers painted in crimson, ochre and green, with the mighty Ausangate towering behind. Your guide shares the geology and culture of the region while you soak in the views and capture unforgettable photos. A warm lunch is served on the way back to Cusco.

This is a challenging high-altitude trek; we recommend acclimatizing in Cusco for 1–2 days beforehand. Horses are available (optional) for part of the route.

## 3. Humantay Lake Trek from Cusco — Turquoise Glacier Lagoon

**Short summary:**
Trek to the breathtaking Humantay Lake, a glacial lagoon glowing turquoise at 4,200 m beneath the towering Salkantay peak. A half-day hike with breakfast and buffet lunch included, plus an optional Andean offering ceremony.

**Highlights:**
- Marvel at the turquoise glacial waters of Humantay Lake
- Stunning views of Mount Salkantay (6,271 m)
- Optional traditional Andean ceremony at the lake
- Breakfast and buffet lunch included
- Round-trip transport and professional guide

**Full description:**
Tucked high in the Andes, Humantay Lake is one of the most beautiful sights near Cusco — a mirror of glacial meltwater that shifts between emerald and turquoise beneath the snow-capped Salkantay. After an early pickup and breakfast in the village of Soraypampa, you'll set off on a roughly 1.5-hour uphill hike to the shore of the lake.

The climb rewards you with an unforgettable panorama: the still, vivid water framed by glaciers and dramatic peaks. Here you can take in the silence, snap incredible photos, or join an optional traditional offering to Pachamama (Mother Earth). After plenty of time at the lake, we descend and enjoy a buffet lunch before returning to Cusco.

A moderate trek at altitude — acclimatizing in Cusco beforehand is recommended.

**Included:**
- Transport
- Breakfast
- Buffet lunch

- Entrance fee

**Full description:**
Get to know the heart of the former Inca empire on this half-day city tour — the smartest way to start your Cusco adventure and adjust to the altitude. You'll explore the historic center, including the grand Cathedral and the Qoricancha, the Temple of the Sun whose Inca walls still support a Spanish convent in a striking blend of two worlds.

From there we head just above the city to four remarkable archaeological sites: the colossal zig-zag walls of Sacsayhuamán, the carved ceremonial complex of Qenqo, the watchtower of Puka Pukara, and the sacred springs of Tambomachay. Your bilingual guide brings each site to life with stories of Inca engineering, religion and daily life — and you'll enjoy sweeping panoramic views over the red-tiled roofs of Cusco.

A relaxed, fascinating half-day and excellent value.

## 6. Classic Inca Trail 4 Days / 3 Nights to Machu Picchu

**Short summary:**
Walk the original Inca Trail — the legendary 4-day trek to Machu Picchu through cloud forest, high mountain passes and exclusive Inca sites, finishing at the Sun Gate at sunrise. Porters, tents, a trail chef and gourmet meals included. The bucket-list trek of a lifetime.

**Highlights:**
- Arrive at Machu Picchu through the Sun Gate (Inti Punku) at dawn
- Hike the original Inca stone trail past exclusive ruins
- Porters and tents — you only carry your day pack
- Gourmet meals prepared by a trail chef
- Official Inca Trail permit and Machu Picchu entrance included

**Full description:**
The Classic Inca Trail is the most iconic trek in South America, and this 4-day/3-night expedition delivers it in full. Over four days you'll follow the original stone pathways laid by the Incas more than 500 years ago, winding through dramatic mountain passes, lush cloud forest and a string of archaeological sites that can only be reached on foot.

Your days are spent walking; your evenings are spent in comfortable campsites where our porters have already pitched the tents and our trail chef prepares hearty, gourmet meals. You'll cross the famous Dead Woman's Pass, explore ruins like Wiñay Wayna, and on the final morning rise in the dark to reach Inti Punku — the Sun Gate — just as the first light breaks over Machu Picchu below. It's a moment you'll never forget.

The Inca Trail requires an official government permit that sells out months in advance, so book early. A good level of fitness and prior acclimatization in Cusco are essential.

**Included:**
- Official Inca Trail permit
- Machu Picchu entrance
- Porters and tents
- 3 breakfasts, 3 lunches, 3 dinners
- Trail cook
- Return train Aguas Calientes–Ollantaytambo
- Professional bilingual guide

**Not included:**
- Sleeping bag (rental US$25)
- Trekking poles (rental US$15)
- Huayna Picchu
- First breakfast and last dinner

**Good to know:** Challenging difficulty · Duration: 4 days / 3 nights · Max altitude: 4,215 m · Max group: 12 · Permit sells out early — book ahead.
